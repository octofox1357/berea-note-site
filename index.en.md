# BereaNote

**Bible notes beside the text.** A study notebook for Mac, iPad, and iPhone.

Write where you read, and jump from your notes straight back to the text. Put the Bible and your notes side by side, and connect verses, notes, and events to each other. The look is calm: black, white, and one Tyrian purple accent, so the words stay in front.

## What you can do

- **Take notes beside the text**: read and write in one window, Bible on one side and notes on the other. Type `/` to drop in a verse block, and the quoted text stays in your note.
- **Link verses, notes, and events as chips**: connect with `@` or `[[`, and follow backlinks the other way.
- **A block editor**: headings, lists, checkboxes, toggles, code, text styles, find and replace, an outline, and comments.
- **Folders and properties**: nest folders, and filter by kind, date, or properties you define.
- **Timelines**: lay out events from Scripture and your research on lines.
- **Priorities**: sort tasks into four quadrants, urgent × important, with due dates, alerts, and Home Screen widgets.
- **Recording**: attach a recording to a note. What you type while recording is time-stamped, so on playback only the line you are hearing lights up.
- A **focus timer**, **password lock**, a 30-day trash, full backup and restore, and Markdown export.

## Your notes stay yours

Notes are stored on your device. There is no account, no advertising, and no analytics. If you turn on iCloud sync, Mac, iPad, and iPhone share the same notes, and they live only in your own private iCloud. Details are in the [Privacy Policy](privacy.en.html).

## Good to know

- BereaNote **does not include any Bible text.** You import a Bible EPUB you already own. Which files work is covered on the [Support page](support.en.html#which-bible-file-can-i-import).
- **Only iCloud sync is an auto-renewing yearly subscription**, with a 1-month free trial. Everything else is free.
- Available in English and Korean.
- Requires iOS 18 or macOS 15.
