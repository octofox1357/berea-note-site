# BereaNote Privacy Policy

Effective date: December 1, 2026

BereaNote is a note-taking app for personal Bible study on Mac, iPad and iPhone. This page explains what happens to your data when you use it.

## Summary

- BereaNote does not collect any data about you.
- There is no account, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party code in the app.
- What you write is stored on your device, and the app sends it nowhere. If you turn on iCloud sync, it is also stored in your own private iCloud database, which the developer cannot read.

## What the app stores, and where

| Data | Where it is kept |
| --- | --- |
| Notes, folders, timelines, properties, settings | On your device, in the app's own storage |
| Audio recordings | On your device. iCloud sync does not upload the audio itself |
| The Bible you import | The app reads the EPUB file you choose and builds a search index on your device. It is never uploaded |
| Backups | A ZIP file written to the place you choose. What happens to that file afterwards is up to you |

The app itself never uploads your recordings or your Bible. Your device's own backups — iCloud Backup, a computer backup of an iPhone or iPad, Time Machine on a Mac — include the app's data, recordings included, as they do for any app. Those backups are made by the system under your settings, not by BereaNote.

## iCloud sync

iCloud sync is optional and off until you turn it on in Settings.

When it is on, these items are stored in the private database of your own iCloud account, through Apple's CloudKit, so that your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account can read them: notes, folders, timeline lines and events, people, property definitions, and the page-lock password record (a salted hash, never the password itself).

The developer has no access to this data. Apple stores it under Apple's own privacy policy.

Locked pages are synced like any other note. The page lock hides a page inside the app; it does not encrypt it.

iCloud sync is offered as an auto-renewing yearly subscription. Apple handles the subscription and payment; the app never receives or stores payment information (card, name or address). If a subscription ends, only syncing pauses and the data on your devices stays.

Turning sync off leaves your data both on the device and in iCloud, and the copy in iCloud stays there until you delete it. To remove the app's data from iCloud, use **Settings → Danger Zone → iCloud Data → Delete…** (this also turns sync off on your other devices; the data on each device is kept), or remove it in your device's iCloud storage settings.

## Microphone

The app asks for microphone access the first time you start a recording. The microphone is used only to record audio that you attach to a note, and only while you are recording. On iPhone and iPad, a recording you have started keeps running when the screen locks or you switch to another app, until you stop it. The app does not upload recordings.

## Page lock

A page-lock password hides the contents of locked pages inside the app. It does not encrypt the files on your device. The password is stored only as a salted hash.

## What the developer receives

Nothing from the app. If you write to the support address, the developer receives what you send. If you have chosen to share diagnostics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may pass on anonymous crash reports.

## Deleting your data

- Delete notes in the app. Notes in the Trash are removed for good after 30 days, or at once if you choose.
- Delete the app's iCloud data as described above.
- On iPhone and iPad, deleting the app removes everything it stored on that device.
- On a Mac, moving the app to the Trash leaves its data folder in place. To remove it too, open **Settings → Info → Save Location → Show in Finder** before deleting the app and move that folder to the Trash (it is inside `~/Library/Containers/com.chan.BereaNote`).
- Backup files you created, and copies your device's own backups hold, are not removed by any of the above.

## Children

BereaNote is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
